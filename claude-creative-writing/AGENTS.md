# Project Agent Guidance

- Use the `creative-writing` skill (`.claude/skills/creative-writing/SKILL.md`) for
  fiction planning, drafting, rewriting, developmental editing, dialogue,
  characterization, worldbuilding, and continuity work. It is discovered
  automatically; invoke it explicitly with `/creative-writing`.
- Preserve the author's intended premise, voice, genre, audience, and constraints. Do not introduce major story decisions without clearly identifying them.
