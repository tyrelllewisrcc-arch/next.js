# Revision Checklist

Use the relevant sections; do not force every item onto every piece.

## Story and Scene

- Does the passage fulfill its intended purpose?
- Does cause lead clearly to effect?
- Does the central character want something in the scene?
- Does resistance require meaningful choices?
- Is the situation different at the end?
- Are setup, escalation, and payoff proportionate?

## Character and Continuity

- Are motivations legible without being overexplained?
- Does each character act within established knowledge and ability?
- Are voices distinguishable?
- Are time, location, injuries, objects, relationships, and world rules consistent?
- Have new facts accidentally contradicted earlier canon?

## Prose and Viewpoint

- Is viewpoint stable and appropriately close?
- Does description reflect the viewpoint character's attention?
- Are abstract statements supported by concrete evidence?
- Are strong verbs doing work that modifiers currently duplicate?
- Do sentence and paragraph rhythms vary with the moment?
- Are metaphors fresh, coherent, and limited to useful ones?

## Dialogue

- Does each speaker pursue an objective?
- Is exposition disguised too obviously as conversation?
- Can obvious statements be replaced by implication, silence, or action?
- Are tags and action beats clear without becoming repetitive?

## Final Polish

- Remove repetition, filler, throat-clearing, and redundant emotional cues.
- Check tense, names, spelling, punctuation, and formatting.
- Replace generic AI-like phrasing with precise, story-specific language.
- Confirm the ending lands on the intended image, choice, revelation, or emotional beat.
